## What the search summaries say (Oct 29-30, 2026)

- Oct 29, 2026: a very large outflow from Binance hot wallets. Early reports said about $2.5-2.8 billion; later coverage says about **$6.3 billion** (TRM Labs: about $6.36 billion across ten blockchains).
- CZ (Changpeng Zhao) posted on X: "User funds are safe! It does look like a hack on Binance."
- Binance said the breach **began with the compromised laptop of one engineer**, which let an attacker submit unauthorized withdrawals. Binance said it found no evidence that wallet private keys leaked.
- **Attribution:** TRM Labs attributes it to North Korea's TraderTraitor group. Chainalysis says it was "likely" TraderTraitor. These are private analytics firms, not an announcement by law enforcement (confirm whether any government has said anything).
- **Response:** Binance suspended deposits and withdrawals, said it will cover all losses, and said user funds are safe. Binance and partners froze about **$3 billion** that may be recoverable. Tether and Circle froze funds. Bitcoin fell about 5%. A proof-of-reserves release was planned for **Nov 2**, and Binance promised a detailed post-mortem.
- One analyst said it does not appear to create meaningful solvency risk (an opinion, not a fact).

## Conflicts between sources (do not state as fact)

- How long the theft took: about 20 minutes (TRM) vs under four hours (another source).
- Number of chains: ten vs nine.
- Total stolen: $2.5-2.8B early reports vs $6.3B later.

## Earlier Binance incidents (better sourced, safe to include)

- **May 7, 2019:** about 7,000 BTC (about $40.7M) stolen, via user API keys and two-factor codes. Binance covered it with its SAFU fund. Sources: CoinDesk, Forbes.
- **Oct 6, 2022:** attacker forged proofs on the BNB Chain Token Hub bridge and minted about 2 million BNB (about $570M at the time). The chain was halted and a hard fork patched it on Oct 12. Much of the minted amount was frozen; one estimate puts the attacker's realized gain at about $137M. Sources: BNB Chain blog, Elliptic, CNBC.
