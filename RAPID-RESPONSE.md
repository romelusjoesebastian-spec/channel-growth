# Rapid-response workflow (breaking stories)

Why: a competitor with ~5.8K subscribers and 2 videos got ~1.75M views and ~5.8K subscribers (about 0.33% conversion) from one Binance-hack video published about 28 hours after the hack. Monetization needs about 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours; one or two hits can do it. At 0.33%, 1,000 subscribers is about 300,000 views.

Channel data (vidIQ): that channel was created Nov 2025 but had 8 subscribers and about 2,000 views until Oct 31, 2026, so it was effectively brand new. Its daily subscribers: Oct 31: 8; Nov 1: 1,970; Nov 2: 4,360; Nov 3: 5,810. The video was published Oct 30 and was slow for about two days before YouTube started pushing it. Don't judge a video in its first 48 hours.

## Target: publish within 30 hours of a high-demand story

| Hour | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 | **Story watch:** confirm the story is breaking and has search demand (vidIQ keyword research, "rising"). Confirm sources exist (official statements, analytics firms, court filings). Decide go/no-go. | 1-2 h |
| 2-6 | **Research** from primary sources. Build a fact sheet with a confidence tag for each fact and the source link. Find the one hyper-specific, surprising detail for the hook. | 3-4 h |
| 6-10 | **Script** using CHECKLIST.md rules: hook, roadmap, one background idea, timeline with exact numbers, twist, debate, unknowns, callback ending. Write a retention map. | 3-4 h |
| 10-14 | **Voice, scenes, render** (check names, volume, scene splits). | 3 h |
| 14-18 | **Package:** title with the stakes, thumbnail that tells the story, chapters named as story beats, description with sources, pinned comment. | 2 h |
| 18-24 | **Publish, then 2-3 Shorts** linking to the long video. Reply to comments. | 2 h |

Total about 14 focused hours, so a rapid-response week uses your whole weekly budget. Don't do it for every story; pick the ones with the most demand.

## Go/no-go checklist

- [ ] Search demand is large and rising (vidIQ estimate in the tens or hundreds of thousands a month).
- [ ] Primary sources exist and say something specific (statement, report, filing).
- [ ] We can add something: a clearer timeline, a better explanation, a debate, or a follow-up.
- [ ] Not a story where a person's guilt is unproven and could be harmed by what we say.
- [ ] Time is free in the next 30 hours.

## Subscriber conversion

- End with a reason to come back that is tied to the story ("when X moves, we'll update"), then a one-line subscribe ask.
- Pin a comment with updates and ask for topic suggestions.
- Link a related video in the end screen and in each Short.
