# Classified Truths: 12-Week Monetization Plan

Starting point (Oct 1, 2026): 2 subscribers, 4 watch hours (365 days), 523 Shorts views (90 days), 4 uploads.

**Goal:** YouTube Partner Program.
- Early tier: 500 subs + 3,000 watch hours (or 3M Shorts views in 90 days)
- Full tier: 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days)

Rough math: 4,000 hours = 240,000 minutes. At ~6 min average watched per view, that is ~40,000 long-form views.
Twelve weeks will not reach this alone. The goal of this phase is a repeatable format with healthy CTR and retention, and the first 100-200 subscribers.

## AI-voice channel: policy safeguards (important)

YouTube's "inauthentic content" rules target mass-produced, repetitive, templated videos. A faceless AI-voice channel is a higher-risk profile for the monetization review, so make each video clearly original:

1. **Original script.** Write or substantially rewrite every script yourself. Do not publish raw AI-generated scripts.
2. **Original analysis.** Each video should add something: a timeline you assembled, a comparison, or a "what this means" section. Do not just retell a Wikipedia summary.
3. **Vary the structure.** Don't use an identical template in every video (same intro, same cuts, same phrasing).
4. **Show sources on screen** (court documents, press releases, news clips used under fair use or licensed).
5. **Disclose synthetic content** with YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting whenever the AI voice or imagery could be mistaken for real.
6. **Use a natural-sounding voice,** and fix mispronounced names, since this niche is full of names.
7. **Accuracy about real people.** Use "alleged" for anyone not convicted. Keep a source list per video. Don't include AI images presented as real photos of real events.
8. **Re-check Studio → Content → Notices** each week for limited-ads flags.

## Phases

### Weeks 1-2: Diagnose and fix
- Review Reach tab per video: impressions, CTR, traffic sources. Record in TRACKER.md.
- Review retention graph for the 14:01 video. Note where viewers leave.
- Add channel banner, About text with a clear promise, and a featured playlist.
- Rewrite titles on existing videos to include searchable names/keywords.
- Publish 2 long videos + 2 Shorts per week from here on.

### Weeks 3-6: Find the format
- Make 8 long videos across 3-4 topic types (scam compounds, financial fraud, espionage, whistleblowers).
- Compare retention and CTR per topic type. Drop the weakest.
- Test one thumbnail style variation (YouTube "Test & compare" feature, if available).
- Start a series name (e.g. "Case File #") so viewers watch the next one.

### Weeks 7-10: Double down
- Make 70% of videos in the best-performing topic type.
- Add end screens and a playlist for each series.
- Aim for first videos above 1,000 views.

### Weeks 11-12: Review
- Compare against the milestones below.
- Decide: continue, change topic mix, or adjust upload length.

## Title and thumbnail rules

- Put the searchable name or term in the first ~50 characters (person, company, case).
- One clear promise, one emotion. Not more than 4 words on the thumbnail.
- Don't misrepresent the video. Misleading thumbnails hurt retention and the channel.
- Avoid graphic imagery in thumbnails (advertiser-friendly).

## Things to avoid

- Sub-for-sub, bought views, view bots. These can get the channel rejected or banned.
- Re-uploading other people's footage or reading other creators' scripts.
- Publishing 5 weak videos instead of 2 good ones.
- Changing the topic of the channel every week.
